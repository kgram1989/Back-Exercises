Back Exercises

A full-body gym plan with two workouts, A and B, of eight exercises each, 3 sets per exercise. Goal: lose fat, build muscle and keep your energy up through the day. Watch each exercise, or press Start workout for a guided session with rest timers and spoken cues.

Seven exercises to strengthen your trunk and keep your lower back flexible. Watch each one, or press Start workout to be guided through them one at a time with spoken cues.

Around the workout

  • Warm up first: 3–5 minutes of marching on the spot, arm circles and a few slow squats (or the rower at the gym). Cool down the same way.
  • Cardio: elliptical, treadmill, rower or bike, 3–5 days a week for 20–35 minutes, on its own or after weights.
  • Flexibility: lower back holds, hip flexor stretch and foam roller.
  • Alternate Workout A and Workout B. Home swaps the three machines for dumbbell and floor exercises.

Before you start

Pick a weight you can lift with good form for every rep, and breathe out as you push or pull. Stop right away if you feel sharp pain. If you have back pain, check with your provider first and keep doing your back exercises.

How often

  • With back pain: 2–3 times a day, or as your provider advises.
  • As you improve: once a day (or more often if your provider says so).
  • To keep pain from returning: once a day.
  • Use a firm bed or the floor.

Before you start

Do the exercises exactly as your provider told you, and don't start until they say it's okay. Stop right away if you feel sudden pain or your pain gets worse.

    Exercises

    Contact your provider if

    • Your back pain gets a lot worse when you do an exercise.
    • Your back pain does not get better within 2 hours after exercising.

    If either happens, stop the exercises and don't restart until your provider says it's okay.

    Get help right away if

    • You have sudden, very bad back pain. Stop the exercises and don't do them again until your provider says it's okay.
